requirments before getting betta (1 week):
stem plants, snails, cycled tank

when getting betta (after fulfilling req):
acclimation with temperature and water dropping, add tannins (waterlog them before adding). 20-30% water change with conditioned water. trim plants

**sand**
-> layer of bacteria break down organic waste, no build up of gases
-> no leeching/ capping of soil
-> easy clean up

**rocks/ decor**
-> beneficial bacteria to latch on
-> boil before adding

**sponge filter**
-> benificial bacteria surface
-> low impact filteration

**water column feeder plants (stem plants/ floaters)**
-> dont need soil/ root tabs
-> liquid ferts/ organic waste in water

**snails (nerite)**
-> aerate sand
-> eat dead plant and algae
-> minimal waste
-> kick off nitrogen cycle (before fish)

**air lump/ filter**
-> for dispersion of nutrients 
-> stagnant water causes nutrient density in places, promote algae and poor oxygen circulation. causes decay and less dispersion of nutrients.
-> circulate o2 for fish
