#aquatic #plants #aquascaping #guide

**yellow water and funny smell**
reason - decaying plants and organic build up. high nitrates. not cycled. could also be leeching from soil
solution - do frequent water change (2-3 days) and use filter. promote water circulation with pump/ airstream [[5.2 - tank setup]]/ substrate vacuum

**dying plants/ lethargic animals after water change**
reason - chlorine poisoning. shock
solution - water conditioner/ leave out water for 12-24 hrs till cl2 evaporate and temp same/ add tannins

**algae**
reason - excess of nutrients in water 
solution - promote root growth in plants to soak up nutrients/ lights out/ cleaning
