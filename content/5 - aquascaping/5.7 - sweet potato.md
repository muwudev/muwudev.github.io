#plants #aquascaping

`ID	Ipomoea batatas
`Type immersed`

`Common Name	Sweet potato`
`Plant Type	Herbaceous, perennial, annual`
`Sun Exposure	Full sun, part shade`
`Soil Type	Well-drained, sandy`
`Soil pH	Acidic`
`Bloom Time	Summer`

suspend potato above tank, letting bottom reach underwater with leaves on outside

potential rot due to toothpick:
- soft, mushy spots around the toothpick holes or on the base
- discoloration (black, brown, or slimy areas)
- foul smell coming from the potato or water

mantainence:
- regular trimming of roots to promote lush leaves
- water circulation/ frequent water changes

note:
- will produce slips that can be planted to grow new potatoes. process takes 3-4 months 
- year round growth (in India)
