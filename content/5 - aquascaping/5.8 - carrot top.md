#plants #aquascaping

`ID Daucus carota`
`Type immersed`

`Common Name	Carrot 
`Sun Exposure	Full sun to part shade`
`Soil Type	Loose, well-draining soil`
`Soil pH	Slightly acidic (6.0–6.8)`
`Bloom Time	Spring (second growing season)`

mantainence: 
- ensure water level remains constantly touching surface of plant
- ensure water level directly below carrot cutting
- check for potential rot

note:
- will not create new carrots, will flower and produce carrot seeds (in 2 years)
- Carrot tops will grow in cooler weather, so it's best to plant in fall or early winter in India.
- Flowering (bolting) happens in hot weather or stress, especially in regions with high temperatures in spring and summer.
- Carrot plants will flower in early spring if they've gone through a period of cooler weather, typically after 8-12 months of growth.
